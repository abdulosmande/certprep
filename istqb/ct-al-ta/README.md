# CTAL-TA mock exams

Interactive HTML transcriptions of the supplied ISTQB / German Testing Board question and answer PDFs. Copyright remains with the source authors, ISTQB and GTB. Source filenames are acknowledged on each page. Tables and diagrams are preserved as local assets; click a figure to open it at full size.

| Language | Syllabus | Sample edition | Main questions | Points | Pass mark | Minutes | Appendix exercises |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| EN | 4.0 | 4.1 | 45 | 78 | 51 | 120 | 8 |
| EN | 3.1 | 2.6 | 40 | 80 | 52 | 120 | 2 |
| DE | 4.0 | 4.0 DE | 45 | 78 | 51 | 120 | 8 |
| DE | 3.1 | GTB 3.1.0 / 1.0D | 40 | 80 | 52 | 120 | 2 |

Main exams offer an optional 25% practice-time extension (150 minutes). Multiple-answer questions receive points only when the selected set exactly matches the source key. Appendix exercises are separate, untimed self-check pages; open-response questions have editable answer fields and source solutions rather than automatic grading.

The `DE/ISTQB-CTAL-TA-v3.1_Sample-Exam-A-Questions_v2.6.pdf` and corresponding answer PDF are English documents and byte-identical to the pair in `EN`. They are represented once in the English catalogue.

Timing and pass marks: [ISTQB Exam Structure Tables v1.18](https://istqb.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/ISTQB_Exam-Structure-Tables_v1.18.pdf).
