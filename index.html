How it works
⏱
Timed
Countdown timer with colour warnings at 15 and 5 minutes remaining. Auto-submits on expiry.
🌐
Time extension
Optional extra time for non-native speakers: +25% on ISTQB exams; CPRE offers 90 instead of 75 minutes.
✅
Instant feedback
Correct answers revealed in green, wrong in red, with a full explanation for every question.
📊
Score summary
Points earned, correct / wrong / skipped count, time used, and a clear pass/fail verdict.
📄
Exact wording
Questions and answers taken verbatim from official sample exam PDFs. Nothing paraphrased.
📱
Works anywhere
Single HTML files. No server, no framework, no login. Works offline once loaded.
